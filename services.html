Our Services

Heavy & Industrial Machinery Brokerage

At Andotra Heavy & Industrial Machinery, we provide brokerage and transaction-support services for buyers and sellers of heavy construction and industrial equipment.

For Machinery Sellers

We help machinery owners and dealers reach potential buyers by:

* Listing and presenting available machinery to our buyer network
* Collecting machine specifications and seller requirements
* Sharing photographs and videos with genuine prospective buyers
* Identifying buyers according to machine type, specifications, and budget
* Coordinating buyer inquiries and communication
* Arranging or facilitating physical machine inspections
* Supporting price negotiation between buyer and seller
* Facilitating communication through the transaction process

For Machinery Buyers

We help buyers find suitable machinery by:

* Understanding their machinery requirements
* Searching our seller and dealer network
* Identifying machines according to specifications and budget
* Providing available machine information, photographs, and videos
* Connecting buyers with relevant sellers
* Coordinating machine visits and inspections
* Facilitating communication and price negotiation
* Supporting the buyer and seller during transaction coordination

Machinery Sourcing

If a buyer is looking for a specific machine, brand, model, capacity, or specification, we can search our network for available equipment.

**Tell us what machinery you need, and we will try to locate a suitable opportunity.**

Machinery Sale Facilitation

If you have machinery to sell, we can help present your equipment to potential buyers.

We can work with:

* Individual machinery owners
* Heavy machinery dealers
* Construction companies
* Contractors
* Industrial companies
* Equipment investors
* Machinery traders

Inspection & Due Diligence Coordination

We can coordinate communication between the buyer and seller for physical inspection and technical evaluation.

The buyer may conduct their own inspection or appoint an independent technical expert before making a purchase decision.

Negotiation & Deal Coordination

We facilitate communication between the parties during price discussions and help maintain a professional negotiation process.

The final purchase, payment, ownership transfer, documentation, and contractual terms remain subject to agreement between the buyer and seller.

Brokerage

Our brokerage arrangement is discussed and agreed with the relevant party or parties before proceeding with a transaction.

We believe that a clear brokerage arrangement and professional communication are essential for protecting the interests of everyone involved.

Our Core Service

Find. Connect. Inspect. Negotiate. Close.
