About Us

 Andotra Heavy & Industrial Machinery

Andotra Heavy & Industrial Machinery is a professional heavy and industrial machinery brokerage business founded by Muhammad Tariq Andotra.

We specialize in connecting genuine machinery sellers with serious buyers and helping businesses, contractors, investors, and individuals source suitable heavy and industrial equipment.

Our role is to provide a professional bridge between both sides of the machinery market. We focus on building long-term relationships with machinery owners, dealers, contractors, companies, and buyers rather than simply arranging one-time introductions.

We operate with an emphasis on professional dealing, confidentiality, transparency, and responsible communication. Before presenting machinery to a potential buyer, we aim to obtain relevant information about the equipment, including its specifications, condition, location, photographs/videos, and seller's expectations.

For buyers, we work to understand their requirements and identify machinery that matches their application, specifications, budget, and preferred condition.

We do not claim ownership of machinery unless specifically stated. As a broker and intermediary, our role is to connect the parties, facilitate communication, assist with negotiation and inspection coordination, and help move a potential transaction toward completion.

Our business covers a broad range of heavy and industrial machinery, including construction, earthmoving, material-handling, agricultural, power-generation, and other industrial equipment.
